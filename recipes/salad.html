Salad

this is a great side dish for when you don't have time and you want something filling, refreshing and healthy.

ingredients

  • lettuce
  • cucumbers
  • tomatoes
  • onions
  • shredded carrots
  • salt and pepper to liking
  • a splash of lime juice

steps

  1. cut up all the vegetables
  2. put all of the cut up veggies in a bowl
  3. add salt, pepper and any other seasoning of your choice
  4. finish it with a splash of lime juice
  5. and the final step is to serve it and enjoy its wonderful taste
